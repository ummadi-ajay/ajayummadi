About

What you will learn

💻

Block coding and Python

Program the micro:bit in MakeCode blocks and in Python, step by step, with no prior experience assumed.

🔘

Built-in features

Use the buttons, the LED display and the sensors, then wire up external parts on pins P0, P1 and P2.

🔊

Add-on components

Connect buzzers, DC motors, servos and an ultrasonic sensor to make your projects move, beep and sense.

🎮

Fun projects

Build a mood indicator, a temperature warning system, a reaction-timer game and, finally, your first robot.

📡

Radio and data

Send messages between micro:bits with radio, and log real-world data to analyse.

🧑‍🏫

For students and teachers

Written for students, hobbyists and educators, with lessons and classroom projects ready to use.

FAQ

Questions

What is this book about?

Getting Started with Micro:bit is a beginner's guide to coding, electronics and fun projects using the micro:bit, written by Ajay Ummadi of Maker Works Lab.

Who is it for?

Beginners who want to start coding and building with electronics. It comes from Maker Works Lab's work teaching hands-on STEM and robotics in Mumbai.

Where can I read it?

Online as a flipbook on AnyFlip: https://online.anyflip.com/aogwo/mjyr/index.html

How is the book organised?

It has 87 pages and 20 chapters in three parts: Getting Started (chapters 1 to 10), Expanding Projects (11 to 15) and Advanced Projects (16 to 20), ending with a glossary of terms.

When was it published?

2025, first edition, by Maker Works Lab, Mumbai, India.
