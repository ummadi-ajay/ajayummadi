Project

LeRobot Training Monitor

A live terminal dashboard for lerobot-train, written in about 280 lines of Python by Ajay Ummadi. It tails the training log and shows what matters while a policy trains.

PythonTerminal UILeRobot
⏳

Progress and ETA

A progress bar and estimated time remaining for the training run.

📉

Loss and gradient-norm plots

Live plots in the terminal so you can see whether training is healthy.

🧭

Trend indicator

A quick signal showing whether the loss is improving.

Credit: built for LeRobot by Hugging Face (Apache-2.0); a separate add-on, not part of or endorsed by LeRobot.

FAQ

Questions

What does the Training Monitor do?

It tails the lerobot-train log and shows a progress bar, ETA, loss and gradient-norm plots and a trend indicator in the terminal.

How big is it?

About 280 lines of Python.
