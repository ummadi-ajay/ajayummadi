MakerWorks Lab: Robotics & Physical AI in Mumbai

MakerWorks Lab is a maker space in Malad East, Mumbai, founded by Ajay Ummadi in March 2025. Innovate. Build. Inspire. Students learn by building, from first robots to embodied AI, vision systems and robot learning.

🦾

Hands-on platforms

LeRobot Arms, Reachy Mini and OpenDuck Mini V2.

📚

Learning tracks

Beginner (ages 8–10), Intermediate (ages 11–14) and a Portfolio Program for Grade 8+.

🏆

Competitions

WRO, VEX IQ, Coolest Projects and Mekathlon.

🎟️

Membership

Ignite, Builder and Dominate passes for makers of every level.
